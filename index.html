Review

Model Notes

  • The unit labels, range limits, and auxiliary values follow the NIST Chemistry WebBook water fluid page.
  • The calculation engine is NeutriumJS IAPWS-IF97. NIST WebBook and REFPROP thermodynamic tables are based on IAPWS-95, so small numerical differences are expected.
  • Viscosity, thermal conductivity, and surface tension use the IAPWS transport/surface-tension releases exposed by the IF97 library.
  • Specific Gravity 20/20C is density divided by liquid-water density at 20 degC and 1 atm, a common lab/instrument convention.
  • Thermal conductivity near the critical region can differ more from NIST because critical enhancement behavior is model-sensitive.
  • Joule-Thomson is estimated numerically at constant enthalpy with a small pressure perturbation.
